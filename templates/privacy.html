Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information or any financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access. You can delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but these providers do not have independent access to your data and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing any privacy-related inquiries promptly and transparently.
